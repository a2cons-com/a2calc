# Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

A2 Calc is made by A2 Labs. In short: **the app collects nothing.**

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting of its own, and it does not send anything over the internet. We don't receive, store, share or sell any information about you.

## What stays on your device

What you type (calculations, the tape, memory letters, takeoff inputs) and your settings are saved inside the app on your device, so they are there next time. They never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

The app reads the clipboard only when you paste, and writes to it only when you copy an answer.

## App stores

Apple's App Store and Google Play handle downloads and updates and may collect information under their own privacy policies, such as crash reports if you choose to share them with developers. We see at most anonymous totals from them (downloads, crash counts), never who you are.

## Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if a future version adds a feature that needs the internet), this policy will be updated first, with a new date above.

Open an issue at github.com/a2cons-com/a2calc/issues.
