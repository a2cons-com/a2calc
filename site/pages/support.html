Support

Questions, problems or ideas for A2 Calc.

Write to us

Email A2 Labs support at support@a2cons.com. Tell us which device you're on and, for a wrong-looking number, what you typed.

Or open an issue on GitHub, where the app's source code lives too.

How do I…

The guide covers typing feet and inches, areas and volumes, memory, and each takeoff. The same help is in the app, under menu › About and help.

Your data

A2 Calc has no accounts and sends nothing anywhere: your calculations and takeoffs are saved only on your device, and deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.
