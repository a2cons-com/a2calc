The calculator

Type it like you read the tape.

Feet, then inches, then the fraction. No converting to decimals in your head.

  • Lengths, areas and volumes: 12′ 6″ × 4 is 50′; length × length is square feet, × another length cubic yards.
  • Round to what you work to: 1/16″, 1/8″ or coarser, with ≈ on a rounded answer.
  • A tape of everything you worked out, and memory letters A to F.
  • Parentheses, squares, cubes and roots, and a physical keyboard on Mac and iPad.
  • Conversions between imperial and metric lengths, areas and volumes.

Stud walls

Drag a window. Watch it reframe.

Lay out the walls one after another with their doors and windows, and see every stick.

  • Studs, kings, jacks, headers, cripples, sills and plates, framed around each opening.
  • A cut list and what to buy in stock lengths.
  • A dimensioned floor plan: tap a wall to edit it.
  • 3D you can turn and zoom, to check it before anyone cuts a board.

Block walls and concrete

One truck. The right truck.

Ever ordered two concrete trucks just to send one back? Get the yards right the first time.

  • Grout for CMU walls, solid or cells at a spacing, plus the cells at corners, ends and every jamb.
  • Lintels over openings, lintel block grouted with the wall or formed and poured.
  • Slabs, footings, walls, beams, columns and round piers, with waste and pump loss.
  • Rounded up to what you order, with the block count too.
