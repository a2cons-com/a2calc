Flooring with section cuts and 3D, and a new Wall tile takeoff.

What changed

- Flooring, under Takeoff: tile and plank laid out piece by piece in any pattern (straight, offset, herringbone, basketweave, hopscotch, hexagon, octagon and dot), from chalk lines A and B, with Best fit. Pieces, cuts, boxes, thinset, grout, underlayment, base.
- Plan / Section / 3D over the floor drawing. Sections are marked on the plan: drag a line to slide it, drag its end tag to turn it, tap the tag to see it. Each section shows the cut wall to wall and both ends at true size.
- Wall tile, under Takeoff: one wall at a time, from the floor or a tub or shower base up to a height. Windows, niches and doors cut out, a level and a plumb line, edge trim, backer board. Elevation with section cuts you drag along the wall, and a section through the wall with its top and bottom at true size.

What to check

- Drag and turn the section marks with a finger; pinch and swipe still work around them.
- The sections read right: walls, base, gap, layers, first piece sizes, the tub and the trim.
- The floor 3D view turns and zooms smoothly.
