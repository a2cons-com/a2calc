New Flooring takeoff: tile and plank laid out piece by piece, with section cuts and a 3D view.

What changed

- Flooring, under Takeoff. A rectangle or any square-cornered room with doorways. Straight, offset, herringbone, basketweave, hopscotch (big and small squares), hexagon and octagon-and-dot, optionally turned off the walls.
- Chalk lines A and B: at the center, a full piece at a wall, a set distance off a wall, or through a doorway; a joint or a piece's middle on the line. Best fit finds the widest smallest cut.
- Pieces counted from the layout (full and cut), slivers in red, the cut at each wall, boxes, thinset, grout, underlayment, base and transitions.
- Plan / Section / 3D over the drawing. Sections are marked on the plan: drag a line to slide it, drag its end tag to turn it, tap the tag to see it. A section shows the cut wall to wall, and both ends at true size.

What to check

- Drag and turn the section marks with a finger; pinch and swipe the plan still work around them.
- Sections read right at the ends: wall, base, gap, layers, the first piece's width, the transition at a doorway.
- The 3D view turns and zooms smoothly, even with lots of small tile.
