History, memory and the other panels close by swiping down.

What changed

- Every panel that slides up from the bottom (history, memory, units, the menu) closes when you drag its top bar down, or swipe down on it once its list is scrolled to the top. A short drag springs back.
- Also in this build (from 0.2.3): paste takes anything with numbers in it, and a fraction next to a length is inches.

What to check

- Open the history, swipe down on the bar: it closes. Scroll the history, swipe down: it scrolls back first, then closes.
- Mac: drag the bar down with the mouse.
